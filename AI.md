# ngon-junk

ngon-junk is a free open-source creative utility for fast 3D model preview, material look development and reliable export passes without a heavy production suite.

## What It Is

Lightweight desktop 3D look-dev tool for model preview, material testing, UV tweaks, image/model export and turntable capture.

## Who It Is For

Artists, designers, motion workers, image tinkerers and YCSWU users who need a small focused utility instead of a large general-purpose 3D suite.

## What Problem It Solves

Modeli hizli onizler, materyal ve UV denemesi yapar, kamera ve turntable ciktisi alir, GLB/OBJ model export ve JPG/PNG/GIF/ZIP ciktilarini tek yerde toplar.

## What It Can Do

- 3D model preview
- material look development
- UV and texture checks
- image export
- GLB/OBJ export
- turntable GIF or frame export
- close-time export confirmation

## What It Cannot Do

- full 3D modeling
- rigging
- animation production
- FBX model export
